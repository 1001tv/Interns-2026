My Review of 1001.tv
Reviewer: Beshar Mowfak Fareed
Date: 16-22 August 2026 (week 1)
1. My First Thoughts
Overall, 1001.tv is a really great streaming service. I tested it on my phone, my computer, and my TV. It has a lot of good content and the website and TV apps are super easy to use. I loved how organized everything is. There are just a few confusing things about the subscriptions and using the phone app, but it is still a strong choice.
2. Finding Shows and Content
Lots of Choices: The app is really well-organized. They have different sections for all the different services and shows, which makes finding things easy.
Great Search: The search bar is amazing. You can type in Arabic or English, and it finds exactly what you are looking for without any problems.
Confusing Subscriptions: One thing that bothered me was that it doesn't clearly tell you what your subscription includes for extra services like TOD. I had to test it myself to figure out how many devices I could watch on at the same time.
3. How It Works on Different Devices
On My Phone
I felt like I had to scroll way too much to see everything on the phone screen. However, my favorite part was casting to the TV. You can easily press a button on the phone to play the show on your TV, and it worked perfectly.
On the Computer (Website)
The website looks fantastic on a big screen and is very easy to use. Because it's a big screen, you don't have to scroll as much, and the sections look really nice.
On the Smart TV
Using the TV app with a remote was great. It's organized almost perfectly for a big screen, and the casting feature makes it even better.
4. Quick Feature Comparison
When looking at the overall feel, the phone app suffers from too much scrolling, while both the computer and TV versions are great and easy to navigate. The search engine is a strong point across the board, working perfectly in both Arabic and English on the phone, computer, and TV. As for sending content to the TV, the phone app casts perfectly, and the TV app receives the video without a hitch.
5. What I Liked & Didn't Like
What I Liked
I really liked that it is very organized with sections for everything. Searching in both Arabic and English actually works perfectly. Playing things from my phone to the TV is super easy, and the computer website and TV apps are well-made.
What I Didn't Like
On the downside, they don't explain the rules for extra subscriptions (like how many devices can use TOD at once). Also, I got tired of scrolling so much on the phone app.
6. Final Thoughts
If you want to watch shows on your TV or computer, 1001.tv is an awesome choice. It is easy to use and finding what you want is quick because of the bilingual search. I just wish the phone app had less scrolling—maybe make different tabs that you can choose from a list on the top of the layout. Also, I wish they made their subscription rules clearer so you do not have to guess which one you are getting on each service. Overall, a great app!
My Rating: 4 out of 5 Stars
