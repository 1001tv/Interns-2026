-One of the main challenges this week was keeping track of the flow of the task while I was writing the code. I understood the requirements and most of the code itself was not too complicated, but because I had to take long breaks between working on it due to exams and other external issues, I would sometimes lose track of where I stopped. When I came back to the project, I would need some time to remember what I had already done, what I was supposed to work on next, and how the different parts connected together.

-To deal with that, I started checking the requirements and my notes every time I came back to the project so I could remind myself where I stopped. I also tried not to rush through the code just because it worked. I wanted to understand what each part was doing and how it connected to the rest of the project, which made it easier to continue after taking a break.
