## Summary

The purpose of this report is to review the 1001 website, mobile application, and Smart TV application from a user experience perspective.

The review focused on design, navigation, ease of use, content discovery, login, streaming experience, language options, and overall usability.

The aim of this report is to highlight what works well and identify areas that could be improved.

Review Method:.

I reviewed the 1001 platforms by using them as a regular user would to see what i would notice first.

To reduce personal bias, I also asked other people who have used the 1001 website and applications about their experiences. Their feedback was considered alongside my own testing to help identify whether certain observations were personal preferences or things other users had also noticed.

The main areas considered during the review were:

Ease of navigation
Design and layout
Search functionality
Content organization
Login and account access
Streaming and video controls
Language options
Consistency between platforms
Overall user experience

lets start:.

((Website Review))

1 Design and Layout

The 1001 website has a modern and visually engaging design all the Movies, series, sports, and other content are presented using posters and thumbnails, which makes the platform feel appropriate for an entertainment service.

The layout makes it easy to notice featured content quickly.

2 Navigation

The website provides access to different categories and sections, and the overall navigation is easy to understand.

However, clearer filtering options could make content discovery even easier, especially for users looking for a specific genres, languages, release year, or type of content.

3 Search

The search function itself works well and is flexible enough to recognize misspelled or incomplete titles which is always a must.

However, the search experience becomes less convenient after opening a result. When a user selects a title and then goes back, the previous search is not preserved. Instead, the user is returned to the home screen and has to type the search again.

This can become repetitive when comparing several related titles. 
an example:. a user looking through multiple Spider-Man movies may want to open each title to check details such as the release year and decide which one to watch first. Having to return to the home screen and repeat the same search each time makes this process slower than necessary.

Adding search history or preserving the previous search results when the user goes back would make content browsing much smoother.

4 Subtitles options

There are not many options available for subtitles or subtitle languages on the website. The subtitles may appear automatically, but users have limited control over changing the language or adjusting the subtitle settings.


5 Language Options

One feature I liked on the website is the option to switch the interface between Arabic and English.

This is useful because it makes the website more accessible to different users and gives them more control over their experience and makes it more Accessible for English speakers.

However, I did not find the same language option in the mobile application. Having this feature on the website but not on the app creates an inconsistent experience between platforms.

Adding the same Arabic and English language setting to the mobile app would make the overall experience more consistent.

6 Trailers

Some of the content I checked had trailers available, while other titles did not. Having trailers available more consistently across movies and series could help users decide what they want to watch. 

It could also be added as a silent preview when u hover over a movie/show, similar to rival streaming platforms.


((Mobile Application Review)) (Disclaimer: I only checked IOS so if there are any differences with Android I couldn't check due to not having Accessibility to one)

1 User Interface

The mobile application is visually consistent with the website and uses posters and promotional images effectively.

The interface is simple and the main content is easy to notice when opening the app.

2 Navigation

The navigation is generally easy to understand.

Users can move between the home page, search, content categories, and account sections without much difficulty.

However, the app could be more consistent with the website by offering the same features and settings across both platforms.

3 Language Setting

Unlike the website, I did not find an option in the mobile application to switch the interface between Arabic and English.

Since the website already provides this feature, adding it to the mobile application would make the platforms feel more consistent and allow users to choose their preferred language regardless of the device they use.

4 Streaming Experience

The video player is one of the most important parts of the application, and most of the main playback controls are easy to access.

However, I experienced an issue when trying to turn subtitles off. The subtitle menu displayed two separate "Off" options, but neither option successfully disabled the subtitles.

This can be confusing for users because the presence of two identical options makes it unclear which one should be selected, and the fact that neither worked prevented me from turning subtitles off completely.

The subtitle settings should be reviewed so that only one clear "Off" option is shown and selecting it reliably disables subtitles.

5 iPad Compatibility 

While opening the app on the iPad I noticed that most of the posters we're very distorted and stretched out to fit the frame.



((Smart TV Application Review))

1 Installation and General Experience

The 1001 Smart TV application downloaded and installed smoothly.

I did not experience any major issues during installation, and the overall experience was good. Navigation through the application was also generally smooth.


2 Navigation Using the Remote

The Smart TV application is controlled using a remote, so buttons and content cards need to be easy to select and navigate.

The overall navigation worked well during testing.

3 Login Process

The main usability issue I noticed on the Smart TV application was the login process.

Users have to enter their login information using the TV remote, which can be slow and inconvenient compared with entering the same information on a phone or computer.

A QR-code login option could improve this experience.


The television could display a QR code that users scan using their phone. They could then complete the login process on their phone instead of typing their information using the TV remote.

This would make signing in faster and easier.


((Strengths))

Several positive aspects were noticeable across the 1001 platforms.

The visual presentation is one of the main strengths. Posters, thumbnails, and promotional images make the platform attractive and suitable for entertainment content.

The search function also performs well when users enter incomplete titles or make spelling mistakes.

Another positive feature is the Arabic and English language option available on the website.

The availability of 1001 across the website, mobile devices, and Smart TVs is also useful because users can access the service from different devices.

The Smart TV application also downloaded and installed smoothly during testing, and the general experience was good.

The Watchlist/Favorites feature worked well during testing. Adding and removing content was easy, and the list stayed consistent across devices, which made it convenient to save content and return to it later.

Another major strength is the content itself. 1001 provides shows and programs that are exclusive to the platform, which gives users a reason to choose the service beyond widely available movies and series. This can be especially valuable for Iraqis living abroad, as it gives them easier access to Iraqi and regional content that may not be available on other streaming platforms in their country.



((Areas for Improvement))

1 Search Navigation

The previous search query and results should remain available when a user returns from a content page.

A search history feature could also make repeated searches faster and reduce the need to type the same title several times.


2 Language Consistency

The Arabic and English language setting available on the website could also be added to the mobile application.

This would provide a more consistent experience across platforms.


3 Smart TV Login

The Smart TV login process could be improved by adding QR-code login. This would reduce the need to enter account information using a TV remote.

4 Subtitle options 

The subtitle options could be improved by adding more settings and more subtitle languages for users to choose from. Even when a movie is already in English, some users may still prefer to watch it with English subtitles for clarity or accessibility.

5 iPad Compatibility

The app could be better optimized for iPad screens. During testing, some posters appeared stretched and distorted, which affected the overall visual presentation, making it not feel as clean as the mobile app/website.

Keeping features, settings, viewing progress, and account information consistent across the website, mobile app, and Smart TV app would help create a smoother user experience.

((Recommendations))

Based on the review, the following improvements could be considered:

1. Preserve search queries and search results when users return from a content page.

2. Add a search history feature.

3. Add the Arabic and English interface option to the mobile application.

4. Add QR-code login to the Smart TV application.

5. Add trailers more consistently and consider short silent hover previews similar to rival streaming platforms.

6. Add more detailed content filters.

7. Continue improving consistency between the website, mobile app, and Smart TV app.


((Conclusion))

The 1001 website and applications provide a visually engaging and accessible entertainment platform across multiple devices.

The platform has several strengths, including its visual presentation, effective search recognition, language options on the website, smooth Smart TV performance, and the Watchlist/Favorites feature, which worked well across devices. 

Another important strength is the content itself, especially the shows and programs that are exclusive to 1001. This can be especially valuable for Iraqis living abroad who may not have easy access to Iraqi and regional content through other streaming platforms.

At the same time, there are several areas that could be improved. These include search navigation, subtitle options and controls, language consistency between the website and mobile app, iPad compatibility, Smart TV login, and the consistency of trailers and previews.

Preserving search results would make browsing easier, while improving subtitle controls and adding more language options would give users more control over their viewing experience. Optimizing the app for iPad screens would improve the visual presentation, and QR-code login could make signing in on Smart TV faster and more convenient. Adding trailers more consistently, as well as short silent previews, could also help users decide what they want to watch.

Overall, the platform provides a strong experience, with several practical opportunities to make browsing and navigation even more convenient for users.
