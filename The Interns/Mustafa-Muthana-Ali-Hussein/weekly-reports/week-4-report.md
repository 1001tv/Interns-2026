Challenges and Solutions

Challenge: Creating the app structure
Issue: At first, it was a little difficult to understand how the different screens and functions should connect.
Solution: I broke the app into smaller parts and worked on each screen and function separately. This made the overall structure easier to understand.

Challenge: Making the note functions work correctly
Issue: Some of the blocks didn’t work the way I expected, which caused problems with the notes.
Solution: I tested each part separately and checked the blocks one by one to find where the problem was coming from.

Challenge: Keeping the app simple
Issue: It was easy to keep adding features and make the app more complicated than it needed to be.
Solution: I focused on the main purpose of Zen Note and kept the design and features simple.
