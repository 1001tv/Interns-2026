Weekly Report - Week 1

During the first week of my internship, I had my email created at 1001 TV and attended my first meeting with Mrs. Amna.

During this meeting, we discussed many topics about the business itself, how it works, the different types of jobs available, and what is needed in the workplace. We were also able to attend the general meeting with all the employees. Through this meeting, we learned a lot of terminology, gained a better understanding of the workflow, and understood the role of each person and their part in the organization.

Throughout the week, we met every day at the same time with Mrs. Amna. We downloaded the app and spent time looking through it to gain a general understanding of where everything is located and what each feature does. We also tested the app to see if there were any errors or problems. Similarly, we looked through the website and tested it as well to understand how it works and identify any possible issues.

The last thing we discussed was creating an account on GitHub. We were asked to create an account so that during the following week, we could begin learning and talking about version control.

Overall, my first week gave me a good introduction to 1001 TV, its workplace environment, and how the business operates. It also helped me become more familiar with the organization, the employees, and some of the tools and technologies that will be used during my internship.
